## Accounts

Synthseek supports two ways to sign in.

**Local accounts.** An email, a username, and a password. Created by an administrator, or by you during first-run setup.

**Plex.** Sign in with your Plex account. Administrators can also import members straight from their Plex server, which creates accounts for people who already have access to the media you are building.

A member can link Plex to an existing local account, and sign in either way afterwards. An account without a password is asked for an email and one before disconnecting Plex, and signs in with those afterwards.

## Roles

| Role        | Can do                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Admin**   | Everything. Manage members, all settings, integrations, engine tuning, jobs, logs, and the review and quarantine queues.                                                                                            |
| **Trusted** | Everything a user can do. Requests are never held for approval, member quotas do not apply, and Lidarr widgets show their releases. They also choose the settings of each request and set their own request preset. |
| **User**    | Search, request, manage their own library and playlists, connect their own discovery and library-source accounts, and set their own profile, theme, language, and notifications.                                    |

A plain member, as these pages call one, has the **User** role. Administrator-only sections are hidden from members entirely, not shown and disabled. The first account created is the instance owner, shown as **Owner**, and cannot be demoted or deleted.

## What is shared and what is not

**Per member:**

- Their own requests. Unless an administrator turns it off, other members see them in the Requests list, except playlists the member made private, with the name unless the member hides it.
- Their own playlists, each one shared with the other members or private. Administrators can still see a private playlist.
- Their own connected accounts: Spotify, Last.fm, ListenBrainz, Plex, Navidrome, Jellyfin.
- Their own discovery mixes.
- Their own Discover board, its widgets and how it looks.
- Their own profile, theme, and language.
- Their own API keys, assistant connections, and music-app credentials.
- Their own notification destinations, and what each one receives.

**Shared across the instance:**

- The music library on disk. Everyone's downloads go into the same library paths, and an administrator decides which path each request goes to.
- The download queue.
- A member's listening, only once that member turns on **Share my listening with the household** or **Show in Now playing** under **Settings**, then **Privacy**.
- Every other setting.

## Privacy

Each member decides what the others see, under **Settings**, then **Privacy**.

Four switches decide where what you play goes. Each has a **Default** setting and a **Private session** one:

- **Keep in my listening history.** Your history, rankings and widgets count what you play.
- **Scrobbling.** Sends what you play to the Last.fm and ListenBrainz accounts you connected.
- **Report to the media servers.** Counts what you play on the media servers linked to your account.
- **Show in Now playing.** Shows the other members what you are playing, under Now playing in their music apps and in Listening now in the player, which also shows the last thing you played. Off by default.

A private session is started from the player's menu, and while it runs the **Private session** column applies instead. It can only turn things off: a switch that is off by default stays off. Until you change them, every Private session switch is off, so a private session keeps, sends and shows nothing. **Private session length** sets how long one lasts before it turns itself off, or keeps it on until you end it.

The rest of the page:

- **Share my listening with the household.** Lets the other members' widgets, and their "Most played (by everyone)" order in the library, count what you play. Off by default.
- **Default playlists privacy.** Whether your new playlists start shared or private. Change one playlist from its menu in the library.
- **Show my name on my requests.** When off, other members see "A member". Administrators see who asked in Requests. Your shared playlists keep your name in the library.
- **Clear listening history.** Removes your plays from the last hour, day, week, or all of them. Plays already sent to Last.fm, ListenBrainz or a media server stay there.

An administrator sets three things for everyone, under **Settings**, then **Members & access**:

- Whether the Requests list shows members each other's requests.
- Whether new playlists start shared or private, for members who have not picked their own.
- Whether music apps show Now playing, and the player shows Listening now, at all.

## The approval queue

Off by default. When **Require approval for members** is enabled, anything a plain member requests is held rather than downloaded.

- Held requests sit in the `pending_approval` state and are not queued.
- An administrator approves or rejects them.
- Trusted members and administrators bypass the gate entirely.
- The gate applies however a request arrives, including when an album or playlist fills in, a library sync, a discovery playlist and an assistant.

Give the gate a purpose by promoting the people you do not want to review to trusted, rather than turning the gate off.

## Download quotas

Off by default. An administrator can cap how much each member downloads, from **Settings**, then **Members & access**.

- **Tracks.** New tracks a member can request within a rolling number of days. Requests waiting for approval count. Retries and quality upgrades do not.
- **Storage.** Space a member's music can take, counting downloads still on their way. A member's own retries and resumes are checked against it too.
- **Whole library.** Downloads stop once the music in the library reaches this size. It applies to everyone, administrators included.

Trusted members and administrators are not limited per member. An administrator can give one member their own limits from the member's edit dialog, and those replace the shared ones.

A request that would go over a quota is refused. Automatic requests, such as a library sync or a discovery playlist, are skipped and logged instead. Each member sees their own usage under **Settings**, then **Account**.

## Managing members

**Settings**, then **Members & access**, administrators only.

- Add a local member, or import from Plex.
- Change a member's role.
- Set a member's own download quotas.
- Remove a member.
- See who owns what.

## Sessions

Sessions last thirty days by default and are stored server-side, so revoking one takes effect immediately. A background job clears expired sessions.

Set `COOKIE_SECURE=true` when running behind an HTTPS reverse proxy.
